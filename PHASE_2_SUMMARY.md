## 💡 Design Decisions Explained

### Why 3-Second Loading?
- Fast enough to not frustrate
- Long enough to feel cinematic
- Memorable without being excessive
- Shows technical sophistication
- Skippable for impatient users

### Why These Navigation Items?
- **About**: "Tell me about you"
- **Projects**: "Show me your work" (most important)
- **Experience**: "Where have you worked?"
- **Skills**: "What can you do?"
- **Resume**: "Official credentials"
- **Contact**: "How do I reach you?"

These answer natural recruiter questions in order

### Why "Systems: Active" Instead of "Neural: 85%"?
- Clearer language
- Looks professional (not sci-fi jargon)
- Understandable status indicators
- Still cinematic with color coding
- Recruiter-friendly

### Why AI Core Stays Centered?
- Represents Albaraa's core (AI focus)
- Breathing motion shows it's "alive"
- Contextual, not just decoration
- Visual anchor
- Shows technical depth
